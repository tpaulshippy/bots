### Understanding Gallifreyan Writing System

Gallifreyan is a fictional alien writing system from the *Doctor Who* universe, used by the Time Lords of the planet Gallifrey. It's known for its intricate, angular design and is read in a specific way that differs from typical human writing.

#### How to Write in Gallifreyan

1. **Direction**: Gallifreyan is read from right to left, unlike English which is read left to right.
2. **Lines**: The lines in Gallifreyan are placed horizontally across the page. When writing, you start from the rightmost line and move to the leftmost line.
3. **Character Placement**: Each Gallifreyan character is placed along these lines. They are usually angular and abstract, resembling stylized angles and lines.
