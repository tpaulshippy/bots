Set up as a parent

1 Parent setup

Install the app on the parent's device

Choose the iPhone app or open Syft Learning in a browser to get started.

Open the web app →
2 Parent setup

Sign in with the parent's account

Use the adult account that will manage bots, student profiles and family settings.

i
Start with the adult account.

Sign in with your Google or Apple account, then use this account to set up the family.

3 Parent setup

Create bots for your student

Open the bot editor to create assistants for different subjects, interests or learning styles.

4 Parent setup

Set a parent PIN

From Settings, choose Set PIN to help keep parent-only controls separate from a student's session.

5 Parent setup

Add a profile for each student

Create a separate profile for every student who will use the family account. Give the profile the student's name and add their Google or Apple sign-in email so they can sign in later.

6 Parent setup

Choose your notification settings

Open Notifications in Settings and enable the conversation updates you want to receive. If your device asks, allow notifications for Syft Learning there too.

7 Parent setup

Install Syft on the student's device

The student can use the iPhone app or sign in through the web app.

Open the web app →
8 Parent setup

Invite your student to sign in

In the student's profile, enter the email address they will use with Google or Apple sign-in. The student session is limited to their learning area.

i
Use the email they will sign in with.

Enter the student's Google or Apple email in the Student sign-in email field in their profile.

Set up as a student

9 Student setup

Sign in with the invited Google or Apple account

On the student's device, choose the sign-in option that matches the invited email. Syft opens the student's profile without the parent PIN or profile picker.

  • Check which email the parent added to the profile.
  • Choose Google or Apple sign-in with that same email.
  • Continue into the student's learning area.
10 Student setup

Choose a bot and start chatting

Select a bot and ask a question, explore an idea or start a study session.

✓
You're ready to learn.

Pick a bot, start a conversation and ask it to help you think through your next question.

Good to know

A few quick answers

My student can't sign in. What should I check?
Confirm the email entered in the student's profile matches the Google or Apple account they are using to sign in.
Why aren't notifications showing up?
Check that notifications are enabled in Syft's Settings and allowed for the app in the device's notification settings.
Where can I get help with a parent PIN?
Contact support and tell us what you need help with. Do not include your PIN in the message.
