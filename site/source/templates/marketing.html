Good to know

Questions families ask

Can I make bots for different subjects?
Yes. Parents can create and customize bots for different subjects, interests and learning styles.
Can my student sign in on their own device?
You can invite a student using the email on their Google or Apple sign-in. Their student session is limited to their own learning area.
What does “tokens per day” mean?
It is an estimate of daily AI usage. The listed figures use Nova 2 Lite as a reference; other models can use the allowance at different rates.
Where can I review student activity?
Parent accounts can review conversations and manage student profiles from the app. The getting-started guide walks through the setup.
